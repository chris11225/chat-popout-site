What the extension does

Chat Popout recognizes supported Twitch channel pages and ongoing YouTube livestreams with live chat. It opens, switches and positions a separate native chat window. Optional features add backgrounds, channel headings and first-use guidance. It is independent of Twitch and YouTube.

Local information and storage

The extension processes relevant page URLs, Twitch channel names, YouTube video IDs, public channel/page metadata, live-player and chat-availability signals, tab/window identifiers and window/screen geometry. It does not read chat-message text or passwords.

Preferences, language, appearance, regular saved window geometry, current/last regular stream control state and first-use markers are stored locally in the browser profile. It does not maintain a chronological browsing history or use Chrome Sync.

YouTube detection records and private control state use browser session storage and memory. Records are removed or replaced on relevant navigation, tab closure or reset; private state is also cleared after the last private window closes. Fullscreen return snapshots are session-only and tied to the managed window.

General settings persist until changed, reset or the extension is removed. Factory reset clears extension settings and control data, not website cookies, browser history or platform accounts. Removal deletes extension-local data. Session storage does not persist across browser restarts or extension reloads/updates.

No developer tracking

The extension sends no analytics or telemetry to the publisher, contacts no developer server, adds no advertising, downloads no executable extension code and uses no cookies API. The publisher does not receive your stream history, window positions or chat messages through the extension. Data is not sold or used for advertising or credit decisions.

Connections to other services

Opening a chat loads a normal Twitch or YouTube page with the relevant channel/video ID. The platform receives normal web requests and may process IP addresses, cookies and account activity under its own policies. Disabling a platform in Chat Popout stops the extension's detection, not the website's own requests.

Optional donations open PayPal only when you use the donation link or scan the bundled QR code. No stream identifiers or settings are appended. PayPal handles payment data under its own rules; the extension receives no payment confirmation and unlocks no paid features.

Service policies: Twitch, Google / YouTube, PayPal.

Private browsing and permissions

Private use requires browser permission. Private stream/control data and geometry remain session-only. Permanent private position saving is disabled; shared non-channel preferences and previously saved regular geometry can still be used.

  • storage: local settings and temporary session control records.
  • alarms: one-shot close/detection deadlines while the background worker sleeps; no periodic tracking.
  • Twitch and YouTube website access: recognize streams, control their native chat windows and apply optional appearance/guidance. No access to unrelated websites is requested.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no analytics, embedded videos, external fonts or tracking scripts. GitHub receives technical requests, including IP addresses, to serve the site under the GitHub privacy statement.

If you email support, the publisher receives your address, message and any attachments you choose to send. The public address uses Apple's iCloud email forwarding to the publisher's inbox. Email providers process messages to deliver them; see Apple's privacy policy. Support messages are used to answer your request and resolve issues, not for advertising. They are deleted when no longer needed unless retention is legally required. Please do not send passwords, login codes or private chat content.

You can request access, correction or deletion of support information by email. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to restriction, objection, portability and complaint to a data-protection authority. The publisher cannot retrieve extension data kept only in your browser; use the extension's reset or removal controls to delete it.

Legal basis and service providers

Where the GDPR applies, processing needed to provide the requested extension functions, serve this information site and answer voluntary support requests is based on legitimate interests in providing a functioning, secure service and resolving user questions (Article 6(1)(f)). You can object on grounds relating to your situation. Information required to answer a support request is voluntary, but without it the request may not be answerable. There is no automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects.

Hosting, email and platform providers may process information outside your country, including outside the EEA. Their linked privacy notices describe their processing locations and transfer safeguards. The extension itself does not transfer your local control records to the publisher.

Funktion und lokale Verarbeitung

Chat Popout erkennt unterstützte Twitch-Kanalseiten und laufende YouTube-Livestreams mit Livechat. Es öffnet, wechselt und platziert deren natives Chatfenster. Optional ergänzt es Hintergründe, Kanalüberschriften und Erststart-Hinweise. Das Add-on ist unabhängig von Twitch und YouTube.

Verarbeitet werden relevante Seiten-URLs, Twitch-Kanalnamen, YouTube-Video-IDs, öffentliche Kanal-/Seitenmetadaten, Live-/Chatverfügbarkeit, Tab-/Fensterkennungen und Fenster-/Bildschirmmaße. Chatnachrichtentexte und Passwörter werden nicht gelesen.

Einstellungen, Sprache, Designs, normale Fensterpositionen, aktuelle/letzte normale Stream-Steuerungsdaten und Erststart-Marker liegen lokal im Browserprofil. Es gibt keinen chronologischen Browserverlauf und keine Chrome-Sync-Speicherung.

YouTube-Erkennungsdaten und private Steuerungsdaten liegen nur im Sitzungsspeicher bzw. Arbeitsspeicher. Sie werden bei entsprechender Navigation, Tab-Schließung oder Zurücksetzen entfernt oder ersetzt; private Daten zusätzlich beim Schließen des letzten privaten Fensters. Vollbild-Rückkehrpositionen sind sitzungsbezogen und an das verwaltete Fenster gebunden.

Normale Einstellungen bleiben bis zur Änderung, Zurücksetzung oder Deinstallation gespeichert. Der Werksreset löscht Add-on-Daten, nicht Website-Cookies, Browserchronik oder Plattformkonten. Deinstallation entfernt lokale Add-on-Daten. Sitzungsdaten überdauern weder Browserneustart noch Erweiterungs-Neuladen/Updates.

Keine Entwickler-Telemetrie

Das Add-on sendet keine Analyse-/Telemetriedaten an den Anbieter, kontaktiert keinen Entwicklerserver, enthält keine Werbung, lädt keinen ausführbaren Erweiterungscode nach und verwendet keine Cookies-API. Der Anbieter erhält über das Add-on keine Streamchronik, Fensterpositionen oder Chatnachrichten. Die Daten werden nicht verkauft, für Werbung oder für Bonitätsentscheidungen verwendet.

Die Plattform verarbeitet dabei eigene Website-Anfragen, IP-Adresse, Cookies und gegebenenfalls Kontoaktivität nach ihren Regeln. Das Abschalten der Erkennung beendet nicht die eigenen Website-Anfragen der Plattform.
